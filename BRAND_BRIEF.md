## Brand

- **Name:** Noor (Arabic/Azerbaijani *nur* = "light")
- **What they make:** home textiles with machine embroidery: decorative cushions, tablecloths and napkins.
  The motifs come from Azerbaijani heritage: carpet (kilim/göl) ornaments, *buta* (paisley),
  and Nizami-era miniature painting.
- **Market:** Azerbaijan. Prices are in AZN, sizes are in *sm* (cm), and the copy is in Azerbaijani (Latin script).
- **Positioning:** premium, heritage/gift-oriented, "modern Azerbaijani table & home".

### Visual language seen in the photos
- **Photography:** outdoor garden scenes in warm afternoon sun, with dark green foliage behind and
  shallow depth of field. Props are a laid tea table, brass/silver jugs (*aftafa/dolça*), pastries,
  fruit, flowers, and white porcelain with gold rims. Hands hold the product, which makes it feel lived-in and editorial.
- **Typography on posts:** a classic serif (Times/Didot-like) in uppercase for the product name.
  It is set in cream/ivory, with the size line underneath and the price large, with "AZN" in superscript.

### Details
1. **Xosrov və Şirin** ("Khosrow and Shirin"). This is the flagship piece. A scene from Nizami Ganjavi's poem,
   in the style of a Tabriz/Safavid miniature, is embroidered on navy linen. It shows two figures, a tree, and a rose bush in a framed window.
   The panel is ivory with gold arabesque borders. The cushion has a brown piped edge. It is the most detailed and expensive cushion.
2. **Göllü** ("with *göl*", the medallion motif of Azerbaijani carpets). It has a chocolate velvet top panel
   cut in a scalloped mihrab-like shape, with a central gold/blue/orange rosette and a border of small pink flowers.
   The base is mustard linen, and a tassel hangs at the point.
3. **Gül Qabı** ("Flower vase"). An ivory tablecloth with colourful floral/arabesque embroidery. The photo shows it
   set with Göllü cushions and a burgundy napkin embroidered with a *buta*. This suggests matching
   **napkins** are (or could be) sold too.
4. **Şirvan Kilim**. A red cushion with geometric Shirvan kilim motifs (hooked medallions, diamonds)
   in beige, silver and white, with a meander ("running dog") border top and bottom. This is the entry price point.

### Collections hinted at
- **Miniature / Nizami series**: Xosrov və Şirin (and possibly more Nizami scenes, such as Leyli və Məcnun).
- **Carpet-motif series**: Göllü, Şirvan Kilim (named after Azerbaijani carpet schools and motifs).
- **Table linen**: Gül Qabı tablecloth, embroidered napkins, possibly runners.

## Website implications (first thoughts)
- **Languages:** Azerbaijani first, with English (diaspora/tourists/gifting) and possibly Russian.
- **Structure:** Home (hero garden photo + story) → Collections → Product page (story of the motif,
  size, material, price, care) → About / the meaning of "Noor" → Contact/Order.
- **Ordering:** most AZ Instagram shops sell via DM/WhatsApp. A "Order via WhatsApp/Instagram" button
  per product is the simplest start. A cart or checkout can come later.
- **Storytelling:** each design has a cultural story (Nizami, carpet schools, *buta*), and that is the strongest
  differentiator. Every product page should carry a short note on where the motif comes from.
- **Design:** an ivory background with olive-gold accents, a serif display font (e.g. Cormorant Garamond /
  Playfair Display) with a clean sans for body text. The site should be photo-led, with generous whitespace.
